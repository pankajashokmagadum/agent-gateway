# rb-02 · Runbook: draining and restoring a server

**Rule:** Drain a server before maintenance and restore it afterwards, keeping at least half of its group in rotation.

## Before
Check how many servers in the same team, environment and tier are already down or draining. A drain that would take the group past half out of rotation is refused (ops-13).

## Procedure
Propose `drain_server` for one host. Wait for traffic to move before starting maintenance. When finished, propose `restore_server` for the same host.

## After
Confirm the server status is up. If a drain was refused for blast radius, restore another server in the group first.
