# rb-04 · Runbook: bringing servers into OS compliance

**Rule:** List out-of-compliance servers by team, start with production, and upgrade inside a change window after draining each server.

## Find
Query the inventory for servers below the minimum OS version (ops-02) and group by owning team. One team usually accounts for most of the backlog.

## Plan
Upgrade staging first. For production, drain one server at a time (rb-02) inside the change window (ops-03), upgrade, restore, and move to the next. Never drain past the blast radius limit (ops-13).

## Report
Send each owning team its list and the date of the next change window.
