# rb-01 · Runbook: restarting a service

**Rule:** Restart one unit on one server at a time, confirm the unit is actually failed or degraded first, and verify its state afterwards.

## Before
Look the server up and check the state of its services. Restarting a healthy unit is a change with no benefit. Check whether a change window is open for the server's environment.

## Procedure
Propose `restart_service` with the host and the unit. If the request is denied, read the reasons: a tier or change window condition names the approval scope an admin must sign (ops-08). Do not retry an identical request without a new approval. Consider whether the same fault can be addressed on a lower tier within your authority.

## After
Query the service again and confirm it reports active. Record what was restarted and why in the incident.
